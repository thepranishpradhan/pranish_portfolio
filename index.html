Customer Behavior Analysis

Personal project · Summer 2026
  • Cleaned and analyzed a 3,900-row customer shopping dataset using Python (Pandas) and SQL (PostgreSQL), loading the transformed data into a relational database to answer 10 business questions on revenue, product ratings and customer segmentation.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard revealing that loyal customers with 11+ prior purchases made up 80% of the customer base, and that male customers drove 68% of total revenue — primarily through purchase volume rather than higher per-transaction spend.
